# Code of Conduct

Onyx4J welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Provide constructive feedback and discuss technical disagreements in good faith.
- Respect privacy, personal boundaries and intellectual property.
- Help maintain a welcoming and accessible development community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and unauthorized publication of private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report violations privately to the project maintainer through an established contact channel. Do not publish sensitive information in public issues.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, warn participants or restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled with appropriate discretion and decisions should be proportionate to the conduct.

This policy applies to the repository and other official Onyx4J community spaces.
